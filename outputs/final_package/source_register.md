## 1. Governance & Methodological Separation

1. **Zero Raw Data Leakage:** All external web research conducted on **2026-11-12** used strictly public cricket knowledge (career/retirement status, franchise rosters, BCCI/IPL 2026–2027 rules, and Rajasthan Royals squad context). No rows, player IDs, match IDs, or dataset-derived aggregates from `data/raw/*.csv` were included in any search query.
2. **Statistical Merit vs. Practical Feasibility:** Statistical rankings in V2 are computed strictly from historical IPL and T20I ball-by-ball records (`2022-01-22` to `2026-11-01`). External facts below—retirements, injuries, BCCI participation rules, franchise trades, and IPL 2027 auction context—are **practical feasibility flags only** and never override on-field statistical ratings.
3. **Explicit `Unverified` Labeling (As of 2026-11-12):** Because the BCCI retention submission deadline is **2026-11-17** and the final IPL 2027 auction register is due **2026-12-03** ahead of the **2026-12-16** mini-auction in Goa, official IPL 2027 retention lists and final auction availability lists are **not yet published**. Every entry's `ipl_2027_availability_status` is therefore explicitly marked `Unverified` with the known pre-deadline factual context.
4. **Evidence Classification Rubric:**
   - `Official`: Direct publications or player/squad profiles from governing bodies, leagues, or IPL franchises (`iplt20.com`, `icc-cricket.com`, `cricket.com.au`, `sa20.co.za`, `mumbaiindians.com`, `rajasthanroyals.com`, `delhicapitals.in`, `lucknowsupergiants.in`, `punjabkingsipl.in`).
   - `Reputable Media`: Established national newspapers and specialist cricket publications (`The Hindu`, `The Indian Express`, `Hindustan Times`, `Times of India`, `Mint`, `Business Standard`, `The Guardian`, `India Today`, `Rediff Cricket`, `CricTracker`, `myKhel`, `SportsTak`, `InsideSport`).
   - `Unverified`: Statuses or claims dependent on unpublished official documents (all `ipl_2027_availability_status` fields as of `2026-11-12`) or unconfirmed media speculation.
