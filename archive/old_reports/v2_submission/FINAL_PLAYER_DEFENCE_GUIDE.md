## 1. Core Defence Pillars (Quick Reference)

1. **Statistical Merit vs. Practical Auction Context:** Every rank `#1–#10` measures **only 2022–2026 on-field delivery value under pressure** (never reputation or post-Nov 2026 contract rumors). Practical availability flags (retirements, coaching transitions, BCCI–PCB restrictions, unverified Nov 17 retention lists) live strictly in Section 9.
2. **Continuous Pressure & Kish $N_{\text{eff}}$:** Using $w_t = \sigma((PI_t - 0.425)/0.08)$ across all $N_{\text{tot}}$ deliveries retains 26,870 intermediate-pressure deliveries (`0.35 <= PI < 0.50`) while calibrating precision via Kish $N_{\text{eff}} = (\sum w_t)^2 / \sum w_t^2 \le N_{\text{tot}}$. Benchmark gates (`tot >= 300`, `hp >= 120`, `hp_matches >= 10`) prevent high-volume low-pressure batters (e.g., Virat Kohli: `hp=114`, yet `Kish N_eff = 621.7`) from entering without true high-leverage volume.
3. **Why Model B Won:** Model B (`0.50 z(ShrunkWP) + 0.30 z(MaintLift) + 0.20 z(ConsEB)` with $\alpha=0.35$ baseline attenuation) removes V1's 1:1 `HP - LP` elite penalty, beats Model A on tighter bootstrap intervals (`36.6 vs 39.3` batters; `44.0 vs 46.9` bowlers) and LOSO stability (`0.905 / 0.937`) even without its `+0.05` `domain_bonus`, and avoids Model C's severe OOS calibration overfitting (`0.653` batters / `0.604` bowlers logistic slopes).

## 4. Tough Evaluator Questions & Crisp Prepared Answers

1. **Q: Why did Virat Kohli (`47`), Phil Salt (`5553`), or Suryakumar Yadav (`1699`) miss your Top 10?**
   - **Kohli & Salt:** Both missed the `hp_balls >= 120` gate (`Kohli = 114` across 2,456 balls, `+5.14` shrunk WP; `Salt = 112` HP / `9` HP matches across 1,643 balls, `+32.89` shrunk WP—entering `#8` immediately under `200/80/8`). Note Kohli's `Kish N_eff = 621.7` comes largely from tail weights on `2,109` low-pressure balls, validating why `hp_balls >= 120` was necessary.
   - **Suryakumar Yadav (`#12`, `score +1.061`):** Ranked `12th` across 1,180.5 $N_{\text{eff}}$ (`657` HP balls) not because he struggles under pressure (`+23.69` shrunk WP!), but because top finishers (`David +41.75`, `Karthik +36.24`, `Naman Dhir +33.57`) beat `+23.69` directly on absolute pressure rate and maintenance lift (`+13.30` vs `+22–34`).
2. **Q: Why is `o3` / `bootstrap_results` showing Sai Kishore at `Top-10 Freq = 37.0%` yet `Median Rank = 6.0 [2–17.3]`?**
   - Because `top10_inclusion_frequency` divides all Top-10 finishes by all `1,000` resamples (unconditional stability including `545` failed-gate draws), whereas `boot_median_rank` and percentiles are computed **conditionally** over the `455` eligible draws where his conditional Top-10 rate is **`81.3%` (`370/455`)**.
3. **Q: Why include 4 retired/coaching players and 2 Pakistani players?**
   - The Round 1 mandate strictly forbids overriding supplied historical data (`2022–2026`) with reputation or future contract assumptions. Filtering them out statistically would bias historical inference; isolating them cleanly into Section 9 preserves **both** statistical integrity and real-world Rajasthan Royals auction utility.
4. **Q: Batting OOS `2022–2024 -> 2025–2026` Spearman is `-0.054` — does that mean batting pressure skill is random?**
   - Ball-by-ball batting outcomes under pressure carry extreme match variance (`runs R^2 = 0.009` in Model C, `RMSE = 1.779`), plus smaller `2025–2026` per-player HP sub-samples (`>=40` balls across `70` shared batters) and role promotions/transfers. Meanwhile **bowling** shows genuine OOS persistence (`rho = +0.152`, `88` bowlers), **odd/even split-half batting correlation peaks at `r = 0.323` (`k=120`)**, and LOSO stability averages **`0.905`**—confirming cross-season descriptive signal even when 2-year forward point-prediction is noisy.
