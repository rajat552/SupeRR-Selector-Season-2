## 1. Governance & Methodological Separation

1. **Zero Raw Data Leakage:** All external research was conducted using public cricket knowledge only (career records, retirements, injury reports, BCCI rules, and public franchise announcements). Under no circumstances were proprietary dataset rows, ball-by-ball deliveries, internal player IDs, match IDs, or dataset-derived aggregates from `data/raw/*.csv` included in any search query or external prompt.
2. **Strict Independence of Statistical Merit:** In accordance with the official Round 1 problem statement, statistical rankings are derived 100% deterministically from `data/raw/*.csv` using the audited V3 methodology. External facts—including IPL contract status, international retirements, coaching appointments, and BCCI bilateral policies—are strictly documented as **practical recruitment overlays**. They do not modify, weight, or override any on-field statistical metric.
3. **Explicit `Unverified` Classification Standard:** Because the official BCCI IPL 2027 retention lists are scheduled for publication on **November 17, 2026**, and the final auction register will be released on **December 3, 2026**, all IPL 2027 contract availability claims are designated as `UNVERIFIED — retention list not yet published` unless verified by official pre-deadline trade or retirement notifications.
4. **Evidence Classification Rubric:**
   - `Official`: Direct announcements, player registrations, or roster profiles published by governing bodies (BCCI, ICC, Cricket Australia, CSA, SLC, BCB) or franchise websites (`iplt20.com`, `rajasthanroyals.com`, `mumbaiindians.com`, `kkr.in`, `royalchallengers.com`, `lucknowsupergiants.in`, `punjabkingsipl.in`, `delhicapitals.in`).
   - `Reputable Media`: Verified news coverage from established national dailies and premier cricket desks (*The Hindu*, *The Indian Express*, *Hindustan Times*, *Times of India*, *Mint*, *Business Standard*, *The Guardian*, *India Today*, *Rediff Cricket*, *CricTracker*).
   - `Unverified`: Claims dependent on pending official BCCI releases or speculative media reports.
